AI Deployment Strategist
Showcase Demo

A live multi-agent pipeline that simulates a complete enterprise AI engagement — from first discovery call to production deployment — built entirely on Mistral AI to demonstrate the core competencies of the AI Deployment Strategist role.

The Problem

Enterprise AI adoption stalls between strategy and execution

Most enterprises understand AI could transform their operations. Few successfully move from that belief to a production-grade deployment. The gap isn't technical — it's strategic. Companies lack a structured methodology to identify the right use cases, build a compelling business case, design a low-risk proof of value, and communicate the vision to executives who hold the budget.

In Spain specifically, this challenge is compounded by regulatory pressure (GDPR, EU AI Act), regional language complexity, and a strong preference for European-sovereign AI vendors — factors that make Mistral AI uniquely positioned, but that require a strategist who can articulate that advantage to every stakeholder in the room.

What This Demo Does

A live simulation of the AI Deployment Strategist workflow

Enter any company with operations in Spain. The pipeline researches that company in real time using Mistral AI, then routes its profile through five specialist agents — each one simulating a distinct phase of a real enterprise engagement:

1
🔍 Account Strategist — Discovery & MEDDPICC
Conducts a structured discovery session and produces the brief an AI Deployment Strategist would share with their AE after a first executive meeting. Applies the MEDDPICC sales qualification framework to identify pain, champion, economic buyer, and competitive positioning.
2
📊 Value Engineer — ROI Model & Business Case
Builds a quantified, defensible business case for the CFO. Calculates FTE savings, process efficiency gains, cost avoidance, and revenue uplift across the top two use cases. Delivers a 3-year investment summary, payback period, and risk-adjusted scenario analysis.
3
🏗️ PoV Architect — Proof of Value Design
Designs a 6-week Proof of Value: technical architecture, on-premise deployment plan (for GDPR/EU AI Act compliance), success criteria, and week-by-week timeline. Funded by Mistral — the customer pays nothing until convinced.
4
🎤 Executive Storyteller — Board Presentation
Writes the 10-slide board narrative using the Burning Platform → Vision → Proof → Path → Ask structure. Addresses the CTO on sovereignty, the CFO on ROI, the DPO on compliance, and the champion on making this their initiative. Pre-empts the three hardest objections.
5
🚀 Deployment Strategist — Account Expansion Playbook
Owns the account after the PoV succeeds. Builds the production deployment timeline, monthly KPI dashboard, executive cadence plan, and 18-month account expansion roadmap — from initial use case to full platform, with a projected 3-year ARR trajectory.

Technical Approach

Built with Mistral AI from first line to last

The application uses mistral-large-latest via La Plateforme API for every AI call — company research, and all five agent reasoning chains. Responses stream token-by-token via Server-Sent Events, so the interviewer sees the AI "thinking" in real time.

Each agent receives the full conversation history of all previous agents as context, creating a coherent chain of reasoning that builds from discovery to deployment — exactly as a real strategic engagement would progress.
